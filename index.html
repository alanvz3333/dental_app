Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Denticore. It explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Denticore mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Denticore provides account registration and login functionality. When you create or use a Denticore account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Authentication information required to securely authenticate your account

Denticore uses Firebase Authentication to provide account registration and login services.

Denticore does not collect or store patient medical records or dental health records.

2. Firebase Services

Denticore uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication – used to provide account registration and login.
  • Firebase Analytics – used to understand how users interact with the application and to improve Denticore.

These services may process information such as device information, app usage information, identifiers, IP address, and other information described in Google's applicable policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Advertising

Denticore uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information about interactions with advertisements for advertising, measurement, and related purposes.

4. Data Storage

Denticore may store certain app-related information locally on the user's device to provide app functionality and maintain application preferences.

Account-related information used for authentication may be processed through Firebase Authentication.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide account registration and login.
  • Maintain and manage user accounts.
  • Provide and improve Denticore.
  • Understand application usage.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.

6. Account Deletion

Denticore provides users with the ability to delete their account. Users can initiate account deletion through the account deletion option available within the Denticore application.

Users may also request account deletion through our web-based account deletion page:

Request Account Deletion

When an account deletion request is completed, applicable account information associated with the account will be deleted, except where retention is required for legitimate legal, security, or compliance purposes.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Denticore. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Denticore is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as necessary to provide Denticore services or as required for legitimate legal, security, or compliance purposes.

When an account deletion request is completed, applicable account information is deleted in accordance with our account deletion procedures.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
